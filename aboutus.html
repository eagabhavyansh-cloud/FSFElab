• Founding & Evolution: The institution originated from the Koneru Lakshmaiah Charities, established as a trust in 1980–81. It started as the KL College of Engineering in Vijayawada, converted into the Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation (KLEF) society in 1996, attained autonomous status in 2006, and was officially recognized as a Deemed to be University by the UGC in 2009. • Core Institutional Vision: The primary mission is to offer a globally relevant learning experience by providing high-quality, accessible, and affordable education. The university states that it blends modern teaching with research to prepare students to contribute to a technology-driven, interdependent world. • Academic & Professional Excellence: The university highlights its Category-I status granted by the UGC, alongside historical high marks and recognition for its focus on innovation, diverse academic streams, and advanced research facilities.
